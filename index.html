For JioPhone · KaiOS

JioPhone — via JioStore

On a JioPhone, apps install from the JioStore on the phone itself — the store has no web link. Artho for KaiOS (v1.0.0) is built for these phones, and its phrases and idioms even work offline.

What Artho does

A proper dictionary, kept simple — built for students, teachers, exam aspirants and anyone who loves the Assamese language.

Two-way dictionary

Look up English words in Assamese, and Assamese words in English — meanings powered by the XOBDO dictionary.

Idioms & phrases

A built-in collection of Assamese idioms and phrases to explore — on KaiOS phones, they work even without internet.

Favourites

Star the words you want to keep and find them again in one place — handy for revision and word lists.

History, on your device

Your recent lookups are remembered so you can pick up where you left off — stored only on your phone, never sent anywhere.

Text size you control

Make the meanings bigger or smaller until they’re comfortable to read — try the demo below, it works just like the app.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics — Artho collects nothing. The only thing it sends anywhere is the word you searched, to fetch its meaning.

One of Artho’s small comforts. Drag the slider and watch the meaning grow — in the app, your choice is remembered on your device.

About Artho

Artho (অৰ্থ — “meaning”) is an Assamese dictionary made in Assam by Saurabh Saha. It started as a simple idea: the meaning of any word, in the language of home, should be one tap away — for a student in a classroom, a teacher preparing a lesson, or anyone reading Assamese after years away from it.

The same dictionary now lives everywhere its readers are: on Android through the PhonePe Appstore, on the web, on JioPhone in its KaiOS version, and inside Telegram as @getartho_bot.
